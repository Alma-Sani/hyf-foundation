# Simple Tomato Garlic Pasta (serves 2)

## Ingredients

- 200 g pasta (spaghetti or penne)
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
- ½ tsp chili flakes (optional)
- Salt and pepper
- A handful of fresh basil (or 1 tsp dried)
- Grated Parmesan, for serving

## Instructions

1. **Boil the pasta:** Bring a big pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta as the package says. Save ½ cup of the pasta water before you drain it.
2. **Make the sauce:** While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes and cook for 1 minute. Don't let the garlic burn.
3. **Add tomatoes:** Pour in the tomatoes, add salt and pepper, and simmer for 10 minutes.
4. **Mix:** Add the drained pasta to the sauce. Stir, and add a little pasta water if it looks dry.
5. **Serve:** Top with basil and Parmesan.

**Tip:** Ready in about 20 minutes. You can add cooked chicken, mushrooms or spinach if you want more in it.
