# Easy Lasagna (serves 6)

## Ingredients

- 12 lasagna sheets
- 500 g ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 700 g tomato sauce 
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp dried oregano or basil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 250 g ricotta (or cottage cheese)
- 1 egg
- 250 g mozzarella, grated
- 50 g Parmesan, grated
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Instructions

1. **Make the meat sauce:** Heat the oil in a pan. Cook the onion and garlic for 3 minutes. Add the beef and cook until brown. Stir in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, herbs, salt and pepper. Let it simmer for 15 minutes.
2. **Mix the cheese:** In a bowl, stir together the ricotta, egg and half of the Parmesan.
3. **Layer:** Spread a little meat sauce on the bottom of a baking dish. Then add a layer of lasagna sheets, some ricotta mix, some meat sauce and some mozzarella. Repeat until everything is used. Finish with meat sauce, mozzarella and the rest of the Parmesan on top.
4. **Bake:** Cover with foil and bake at 190°C for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake 15 more minutes, until golden and bubbly.
5. **Rest:** Let it sit for 10 minutes before cutting.

**Tip:** If your sheets are the "no-boil" kind, you can use them straight from the box. Otherwise, boil them first according to the package directions.
